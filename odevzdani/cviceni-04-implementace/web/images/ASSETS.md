# Prague Pulse Fest artwork

The wordmark, favicon, signal, orbits and star illustrations were created specifically for Prague Pulse Fest. The current logo is typographic; older sound-wave graphics are historical unused assets. Current photographs and artwork are documented in `PHOTO-CREDITS.md`. They do not use Rock for People logos, artwork or brand assets.

Fonts are self-hosted WOFF2 files from the official Fontsource npm packages `@fontsource/barlow-condensed@5.3.0` (Barlow Condensed, weights 800 and 900) and `@fontsource/dm-sans@5.3.0` (DM Sans, weights 400 and 700). Both are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Full license texts are distributed in `fonts/`. Latin and Latin Extended subsets support Czech characters. No external font services are called by the website.

`festival-crowd.png` is an original AI-generated illustrative concert image created for this project. It is a visualisation, not documentary photography of an actual festival venue.
