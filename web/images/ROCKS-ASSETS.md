# Prague Pulse Fest visual assets

- `prague-pulse-logo.svg` and `prague-pulse-logo-light.svg`: original outlined wordmarks composed specifically for this fictional festival. Letter outlines come from the project's self-hosted Barlow Condensed 900 (SIL Open Font License 1.1; full license in `fonts/`). The current lettering arrangement is original and contains no sound-wave symbol. No Rock for People logo, font file or artwork was copied.
- `rocks-mark.svg`: original geometric music/lighting illustration created for the site.
- `festival-atmosphere.webm`: silent 10-second 1280 × 720 VP9 animation, 24 frames per second, made with FFmpeg from this project's existing original AI-generated `festival-crowd.png`. Slow, seamless panning and zooming animate a still image. It is illustrative animation, not filmed festival footage or an accurate representation of the real Výstaviště site. Original image provenance is in `ASSETS.md`.

The video and concert poster above belong to a previous version and are no longer used in the homepage hero. Current photographic and illustrative assets are documented in `PHOTO-CREDITS.md`. No external video player, stream, tracker or CDN is used at runtime. The old fictional-performer image provenance remains separately in `performers/README.md`.
